## Visual Aesthetic Guidelines
- **Palette:** Warm ivory (#FAF7F3), cream/linen, soft champagne, muted blush, and deep plum (#70465B).
- **Lighting:** Soft natural studio lighting, warm diffused key light, clean minimal background.
- **Subjects:** Diverse adult models, realistic skin textures with natural pores, neat cuticles, realistic hair strand definition and volume.
- **Composition:** Close-up and mobile-first framing, no text, no watermarks, no artificial distortion.
